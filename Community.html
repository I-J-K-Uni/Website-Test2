What is the Gamer dude community?

The gamer dude community is a place where people can post about their favourite topics of the month. Every month the topic changes and this month the topic is about people's favourite sound designs from a game.

Why is important to have a community?

Here at Gamer Dudes, we believe that building a community allows for people to be more intereseted in the games that they purchase as they can see other people's feedback from the games which can help them decide what game they want to purchase
